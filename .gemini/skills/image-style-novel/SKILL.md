# Image Style: Modern Graphic Novel

When generating or editing images, always follow these design instructions to maintain a consistent high-quality graphic novel aesthetic.

## Design Instructions

- **Style**: Semi-realistic digital illustration in a modern, high-quality graphic novel style.
- **Outlining**: Strong, dark outlining on all primary subjects.
- **Texture & Shading**: Extensive use of cross-hatching for texture and shading.
- **Lighting**: Dramatic, directional lighting with sharp shadows and bright rim lights.
- **Color Palette**: Sophisticated color palette featuring strong warm/cool contrast.
- **Composition**:
    - Keep the main subject crisp and highly detailed in the foreground.
    - Blur or soften the background to create a cinematic depth of field.

## Tool Integration

- `mcp_nanobanana_generate_image`
- `mcp_nanobanana_edit_image`
- `mcp_nanobanana_generate_icon`
